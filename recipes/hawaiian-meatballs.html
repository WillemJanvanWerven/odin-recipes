Hawaiian Meatballs

Home

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup pineapple juice
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, combine the ground pork, breadcrumbs, egg, and garlic.
  2. Add the pineapple juice, soy sauce, and honey. Mix well.
  3. Shape the mixture into meatballs.
  4. Bake in a preheated 375°F (190°C) oven for 20-25 minutes, or until cooked through.
