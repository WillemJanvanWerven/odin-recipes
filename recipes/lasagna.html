Lasagna

Home

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  3. In a skillet, brown the ground beef over medium heat. Drain excess fat.
  4. Add the marinara sauce to the cooked beef and simmer for 5 minutes.
  5. In a baking dish, layer noodles, meat sauce, ricotta cheese, and mozzarella cheese. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with a layer of mozzarella cheese on top.
  6. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes, or until cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  7. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before serving.
